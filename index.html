Privacy policy

LikPay keeps your data on this phone only.

What is stored on the phone

Verified payments, cash sales, the transaction codes you have already scanned, your saved accounts, your language and time window, and the clock correction. All of it stays in the app's private storage on this phone.

What leaves the phone

When you check a receipt, its transaction code (or the QR link) is sent to the bank's official receipt service, Telebirr or CBE, to verify it. A small time request goes to Google or Cloudflare to correct the phone's clock. Nothing else is sent anywhere, and we never see your data.

Camera

The camera is used only to read receipts. No photos or videos are saved.

No account

No account, no phone number, no sign up, no ads.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app. Everything it stored is removed with it.
